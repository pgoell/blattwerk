# Tutorials

These tutorials are for Claude, in English. One is for teachers, in German: point a teacher to it when she asks how to begin.

* [Your first sheet](erstes-blatt.md): For Claude. A heading, a name line and twelve plus sums to 20 with a seed, placed one under the other and checked with a preview.
* [A week of Lernwörter sheets](eine-woche-lernwoerter.md): For Claude. One Montag sheet with a word list and a trace ruling, copied for Dienstag to Freitag, each copy with its own words.
* [A maths sheet with an answer key](rechenblatt-mit-loesungen.md): For Claude. A minus sheet to 20 with a gap format, and a Selbstkontrolle in mixed form that checks its results, then what the teacher does for the answer key and the variants.
* [Dein erstes Blatt mit Claude](dein-erstes-blatt-mit-claude.md): For teachers, in German. From a Blattomat account to a first worksheet that Claude makes, in short steps.
