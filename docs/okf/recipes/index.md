# Recipes

Whole sheets for common tasks. Each recipe is a doc that passes the schema; start from it and change the words, the seed or the text.

* [Plusaufgaben bis 20 mit Selbstkontrolle](plusaufgaben-bis-20.md): A heading, a name line, twelve addition sums to 20 with a fixed seed, and a self-check that lists the results shuffled.
* [Lernwörter mit Lineatur und Silbenbögen](lernwoerter-silbenboegen.md): Four Lernwörter on a two-line ruling, each word in grey to write over with a bow under each syllable.
* [Uhrzeiten lesen](uhrzeiten-lesen.md): A heading, a name line, and six clocks with full and half hours to read, the time written under each.
* [Zahlenmauern](zahlenmauern.md): A heading, a name line, and four Zahlenmauern with the sums above the given foot stones to fill in.
* [Ein Bild zeichnen und platzieren](bild-zeichnen.md): A heading, a name line, one simple line drawing made as SVG and uploaded, and three labels to write on the picture.
* [Suchsel mit Tieren](suchsel-tiere.md): A heading, a name line, and a word search of five animal words, running down, with the words printed under the grid.
* [Sprechblase und Rahmen](formen-sprechblase.md): A heading, a name line, a speech bubble with a question inside, and a frame to write the answer in.
* [Namenszeile und Punkte](namenszeile-punkte.md): A heading, a name line, a text task with its points box beside it, and a total points box at the foot.
* [Symbole mit Aufgaben](symbole-aufgaben.md): A heading, a name line, and three OpenMoji symbols, each next to a short task.
* [Wochentage als Tabelle](wochentage-tabelle.md): A heading, a name line, and a table with the weekdays Montag to Freitag in a bold head row.
* [Rechendreiecke](rechendreiecke.md): A heading, a name line, and four Rechendreiecke with some fields given and the rest to fill in.
* [Zahlenhäuser](zahlenhaeuser.md): A heading, a name line, and four Zahlenhäuser with the left box of each floor given and the rest to fill in.
* [Zahlenketten](zahlenketten.md): A heading, a name line, and four Zahlenketten of six rings with the first two rings given and the rest to fill in.
* [Pfeilbilder](pfeilbilder.md): A heading, a name line, and four Pfeilbilder of four boxes with the start number and the operators given and the other boxes to fill in.
* [Operatorfelder](operatorfelder.md): A heading, a name line, and four Operatorfelder of four boxes with the start number and the operators given and the other boxes to fill in.
* [Rechenräder](rechenraeder.md): A heading, a name line, and four Rechenräder of two rings with the centre's number given and the outer ring to fill in.
* [Rechentabellen](rechentabellen.md): A heading, a name line, and a Maltabelle of the 2er, 5er and 10er Reihe with the row values given and the result cells to fill in.
* [Magische Quadrate](magische-quadrate.md): A heading, a name line, and two 3 × 3 Magische Quadrate bis 20 with the Zielzahl shown and four cells given.
* [Malquadrate](malquadrate.md): A heading, a name line, and two Malquadrate for the Einmaleins with the factors given at the edges and the products to fill in.
* [Zerlegungsfelder](zerlegungsfelder.md): A heading, a name line, and six Zerlegungsfelder bis 20 for the Zehnerübergang in two steps, with the first and the second number given and the split and the result to fill in.
* [Halbschriftlich rechnen](halbschriftlich.md): A heading, a name line, and six Plusaufgaben bis 1000 for the halbschriftliche Rechnen with the Zehnerübergang in the Einer step, the second number split place by place, with the first and the second number given and the steps and the result to fill in.
* [Zahlenstrahl bis 100](zahlenstrahl-bis-100.md): A heading, a name line, and a Zahlenstrahl from 0 to 100 with boxes to fill in.
* [Hunderterfeld](hunderterfeld.md): A heading, a name line, and a Hundertertafel with twenty numbers hidden for the pupil to fill in.
* [Ein Blatt mit zwei Seiten](seiten.md): Two pages: a heading, a name line and a text task on page 1, then a heading and a ruling on page 2.
* [Ein Blatt quer](quer.md): A sheet turned landscape, with a heading and a table across its full width.
* [Hilfslinien setzen](hilfslinien.md): Two columns of text that line up on the sheet's guides at 15, 105 and 195 mm.
* [Lernwörter verknüpfen](lernwoerter-verknuepfen.md): Five Lernwörter in the sheet's list, a ruling that copies them, and a text that blanks out their vowels.
* [Silbenbögen und Silbenfarbe](silbenboegen.md): A text with a bow under each syllable, the syllables in two colours.
* [Varianten zum Drucken](varianten.md): A maths block and a suchsel with seeds, so each printed variant gets its own tasks and grid.
* [Lückentext mit Lösungen](loesungen.md): A text with four gaps for the pupil, a word box under it, and the answer key shown for the teacher.
