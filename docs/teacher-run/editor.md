## How the editor works

- `Neues Blatt` on `Meine Blätter` opens an empty sheet in the editor. The logo at the top left leads back to `Meine Blätter`.
- After `Neues Blatt` or a click on a sheet, the page first shows the editor's frame with an empty white page, and after a moment the word `Lädt` on it: the sheet is loading. In the MCP page, `wait_for` the text `Gespeichert` before the first snapshot of a sheet, and the text `Neues Blatt` before the first snapshot of `Meine Blätter`. The answer of `wait_for` is a snapshot of the whole page, and a long one: use it, and take no `take_snapshot` right after it. On a sheet with a Lernwörter list, that answer is very long (a row repeats each word some 20 times): there, `take_snapshot` with a `filePath` writes the page to a file, and `grep` searches the file. In the tool's window `last.png` may still show the page before: run `where "Gespeichert"` or `where "Neues Blatt"` until it prints a match, then `screenshot`.
- A snapshot's uids change after every menu, tab or new block. Take a fresh snapshot before each click by uid.
- The tour (`Rundgang`) opens the first time the editor opens on a device, as the app remembers that in the browser. Both windows start with an empty browser, so the tour opens in each on its first sheet. Close it with `Beenden`. To see it again, click `Rundgang` in the bar (it may lie under `Mehr`).
- The window is 1280 px wide. The bar at the top holds the tabs `Start`, `Ansicht` and `Vorlagen`, the field `Titel`, `Rückgängig`, `Wiederholen`, and at the right `Lösungen` and `PDF`. The left panel starts with `Mehrere`, which lets you pick several blocks, and then holds what you can put on the sheet: `Text`, `Überschrift`, `Bild`, `Symbol`, `Tabelle`, `Lineatur`, `Namenszeile`, `Punkte`, `Rechnen`, `Strecke`, shapes, and `Neue Seite`. The right panel shows the settings of the block you picked; the button `Format und Ansicht` shows and hides it. If the bar has no tabs, the button `Seiten und Vorlagen` opens pages and templates, and the right panel has the tabs `Format` and `Ansicht`.
- Many buttons are icons. Find a control by its name in `take_snapshot`. A screenshot of the MCP page never shows a name: that Chrome has no mouse, and the app shows an icon's name only to a mouse. In the tool's window `hover "<name>"` or `hover X,Y` shows the name of an icon in the bar or the left panel as a teacher sees it: read `last.png`. What a pull request claims "with a mouse", try in the tool's window.
- A new block lands under the lowest block of the page, 5 mm below it, at that block's left edge, when it fits above the margin, and it is picked. The first block on an empty page lands at the top, in the margin. Place the blocks below one another in the order of the recipe, so that none overlaps. The plain way, in the MCP page: `fill` the field `Y` of the right panel with the millimetres from the page's top, then `press_key` Enter. In the tool's window the same is `click "Y"`, `key Control+a`, `type "40"`, `key Enter`, for 40 millimetres. `Y` stands in the panel only while a block is picked: after an Escape that unpicked it, `click "Y"` finds nothing, and the next `Control+a` picks every block on the page. Pick the block again first. An upright page is 297 high, and `Höhe` beside it is the block's own height. In the tool's window, `drag X,Y X2,Y2` from the block's middle moves it as a teacher does.
- The arrow keys move the picked block by 1 mm, but only while no field of the right panel has the focus: in `Y`, up and down change the number. One Escape in a number field gives the keys back to the sheet and the block stays picked. After a choice from a list (`Zahlenraum`), do not press Escape: it unpicks the block. The choice gives the keys back to the sheet by itself, so the arrows move the block at once. In a list, `End` goes to its last entry, which for `Zahlenraum` is `Eigener`, not the largest number. A click that misses a list leaves the arrow keys moving the picked block. A second Escape picks nothing, and so does a click on an empty part of the sheet.
- A new `Text` or `Überschrift` is open for typing. Type, then press Escape. A double click opens a text, a table cell or a Lineatur again, and so does F2 on the one picked block. A double click on a stone of a Zahlenmauer opens that stone on the sheet: type the number there. The small wall in the right panel is the other way.
- The page thumbnails are in the left panel of the tab `Vorlagen`, under the heading `Seiten`. The tab `Start` holds what you put on the sheet and has no thumbnails. Click `Vorlagen`, click the thumbnail (it reads `Seite 2`), then `key Control+ArrowDown` moves that page down one place and `key Control+ArrowUp` moves it up. `key Control+Shift+ArrowDown` moves it to the end and `key Control+Shift+ArrowUp` to the start. The badge `Seite 2 von 3` at the lower right of the desk only names the page in view. It is not a button: a click on it opens nothing, and it does not open the thumbnails. Where the bar has no tabs, the button `Seiten und Vorlagen` opens the same thumbnails.
- A table has no `Drehung`: the field is greyed, and so are `Rechtsdrehung 90°` and `Linksdrehung 90°`. A table stays level. That is how the app works, not a fault to report. `Schneidelinien` still turns the dashed cut lines on, on that level table. A turned table with cut lines cannot be made.
- The sheet saves by itself. The editor's bar says `Gespeichert` when it has; no other page shows that word, so `where "Gespeichert"` finds it in the editor only. After a change the bar says `Speichert …` first. A `Gespeichert` read right after a change may be the old one: open the sheet again and check that the next screenshot shows the change.
- Set each sheet's name in `Titel` before you export: the PDF file takes that name. In the MCP page, click `Titel` by its uid in `take_snapshot`, then `press_key` Control+A, `type_text` the name, `press_key` Enter. Do not `fill` it: `fill` adds to the text that stands there, so the name would follow `Unbenanntes Blatt`. In the tool's window: `click "Titel"`, `key Control+a`, `type "<the name>"`, `key Enter`.
- Only the tool's window can do these:
  - pick two blocks: `click X,Y` on the first, `shift-click X,Y` on the second;
  - a slider: `click "Randstärke"`, then `key ArrowRight 3`. The click puts the slider at its middle, each arrow moves it one step. If the slider lies outside the window, the tool turns the panel to it by itself;
  - a control of the right panel below the window's lower edge, such as the slider `Randstärke` or the buttons `Jedes n-te Wort` and `Lösungswörter`: `click` it, and the tool turns the panel to it. `wheel` is needed only for a point given as `X,Y`, or to bring something into view for a screenshot, as `wheel X,Y 400` with X,Y read off the screenshot;
  - a colour, with the block picked: `click "Füllung"` (or `Farbe`, `Rand`), then `key ArrowUp 60`, `key ArrowRight 60`, `key Enter`. The picker is a window of its own that the mouse cannot reach: keys only. One arrow is a step too small to see: 60 up make the colour lighter, 60 to the right give it a tint. From pure black the arrows to the right alone change nothing, and a colour as light and as strong as it can be (`#ff0000`) moves with `ArrowDown` and `ArrowLeft` only. A changed border is hard to tell in a picture: read the colour before and after, with `value "Rand"` (or `value "Füllung"`, `value "Farbe"`);
  - a list that must be seen open (`Art der Lineatur`, `Schriftart`): `click` it, `key ArrowDown`, `key Enter`.
  - an option of an open list: `click X,Y` on it does nothing. A native list is drawn outside the page. `click` the list, `key ArrowDown`, `key Enter`. `click "bis 100"` finds nothing. A font list is on the page: `click` its name, as `click "Druckschrift Nord"`.
  - the numbering under the heading `Nummerierung`: the buttons read `1 2 3` and `a b c`, with the spaces. `click "1 2 3"` sets numbers and `click "a b c"` sets letters. `click "123"` finds nothing.
  - hold Space and drag, which pans the desk: `key-drag Space X,Y X2,Y2`. `key Space` leaves the desk where it is.
- A block on the sheet has no name in either window. The MCP page's snapshot does not list it, so a click there cannot pick it. In the tool's window, `click X,Y` on the block, the place read off the screenshot. A new block is already picked. The Hunderterfeld's grid of squares shows in the right panel once that block is picked and `Ausschnitt` is ticked, or `Färben` is `Selbst färben`, or `Zahlen` is `Selbst verdecken`. Each square is named `Feld 1` through `Feld 100`, or `Feld 0` through `Feld 99` when `Erste Zahl` is `0 bis 99`. `where "Feld 100"` prints its place.
- A file from the desktop, in the tool's window: `drop bild.png X,Y` drops `/tmp/teacher-run/bild.png` on the sheet at X,Y. The `Bild` button is the other way in.
- `Abmelden` lies on `Konto` only. To sign out from another page, `open /konto` first; to end the sign-in where you are, use `expire`.
- After any sign in, the window stays on the path it showed: a sign in after `Abmelden` on `Konto` stays on `/konto`, and one after `expire` stays on the sheet. `open /` leads to `Meine Blätter`.
- A gap of two words: `key Enter` opens the text, `key Home` puts the caret at its start, `key Control+Shift+ArrowRight 2` picks two words, then `click "Lücke"` makes them a gap. Pick the words first: without a pick, `Lücke` gaps the word under the caret or the whole block.
- Several pages that are not a range: `where "Seite 3"` prints the thumbnail (its words include the page's own, so the name alone is not `Seite 3`), then `ctrl-click X,Y` adds it and a second `ctrl-click` on the same place drops it. `shift-click` picks every page from the one in use to the one clicked.
- A finger held still: `finger X,Y` on a block or a thumbnail. The menu opens when the finger lifts. A finger that moves is `swipe`, and that opens no menu.
- Words as Word sends them: `paste-word "Ein *fett* Wort"`. `*fett*` is bold and `_schief_` is italic. A line that starts with `- ` is a bullet.
- A passage from a web page: the live site is barred. Copy the stage's own page. `open /datenschutz`, `key Control+a`, `key Control+c`, then on the sheet `key Control+v`.
- A picture dragged off a web page: `drop-web bild.png X,Y` lands the file as that picture. An address, `drop-web https://example.org/katze.png X,Y`, is not fetched.
- A voice note: `mic`, then `click "Feedback"` and `click "● Aufnahme starten"`. The MCP page has no microphone. `key Escape` while it records keeps the clip.

## Two teachers

What one account copied must not reach the next account on the same computer: not in a text, not on the sheet, not in another app. Check it in the tool's window, where the clipboard is the window's own:

1. As the first teacher, on a sheet: pick a block, or words in an open text, and `key Control+c`. `paste-elsewhere` shows what the clipboard holds.
2. `where "Gespeichert"` until it prints a match: before that, `open` meets the box that asks about unsaved work. Then `open /konto`, `click "Abmelden"`. The form `Anmelden` shows.
3. Sign in as the second teacher: `click "E-Mail"`, `type "teacher-run-2@stage.invalid"`, `click "Passwort"`, `type "<the second password>"`, `key Enter`. The window stays on `/konto`: `open /`, `where "Neues Blatt"` until it prints a match.
4. `click "Neues Blatt"`, `click "Beenden"` if the tour opens, then paste each way a teacher would: `key Control+v` on the sheet, `click "Einfügen"`, `key Control+v` in an open text, and `paste-elsewhere`. None may bring what the first teacher copied.
5. Sign out and sign in as the first teacher again, the same way, and `open /`.

The MCP page stays signed in as the first teacher all along.
